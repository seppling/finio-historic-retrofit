# Fineo / vacuum glass for US historic window retrofit

Research notes on whether there is a business in retrofitting vacuum insulated
glass (VIG) such as AGC's **Fineo** into existing American wood windows, as a
restoration-plus-reglazing service and as a channel into historic tax credit
projects.

- [`opportunity-memo.md`](opportunity-memo.md) – the assessment, with sources.

Prompted by Brent Hull's video *"Why I Hate Insulated Glass and The New Great
Solution for Traditional Windows"* (Sept 2026), 100yearwindow.com and
fineoglass.eu.
